// HOW IT WORKS

Four steps.
Zero compromise.

Every message travels through Stellar and IPFS — encrypted before it leaves your device. No server ever sees your plaintext.

01
Generate your keys

A 12-word BIP39 mnemonic generates your Stellar address and NaCl encryption keys. Same phrase, any device, forever.

02
Encrypt on device

Your message is encrypted using NaCl box encryption before it leaves your phone. The relay never sees plaintext.

03
Send via Stellar

The encrypted blob is stored on IPFS. A Stellar transaction carries the pointer to your recipient. Cost: 0.00001 XLM.

04
Receive and decrypt

Recipient fetches the pointer, retrieves from IPFS, and decrypts locally. Only they can read it.

Built different.

Everything Signal and Telegram won't give you.

Zero identity required

No phone number. No email. No real name. Your identity is a 12-word phrase and an @username you choose.

Find by @username

Search any user by their @username. No address copy-pasting, no QR scanning required.

Send XLM in chat

Send Stellar (XLM) directly inside any conversation. Money and messages in one encrypted thread.

Decentralized storage

Messages live on IPFS — not on our servers. We cannot delete them. We cannot read them.

Near-zero cost

Each message costs 0.00001 XLM — roughly $0.000003. Send thousands of messages for less than a cent.
