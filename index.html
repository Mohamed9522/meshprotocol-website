// HOW IT WORKS

Four steps.
Zero compromise.

Every message travels through Stellar and IPFS — encrypted before it leaves your device. No server ever sees your plaintext.

01
🔑
Generate your keys

A 12-word BIP39 mnemonic generates your Stellar address and NaCl encryption keys. Same phrase, any device, forever.

02
🔒
Encrypt on device

Your message is encrypted using NaCl box encryption before it leaves your phone. The relay never sees plaintext.

03
⛓️
Send via Stellar

The encrypted blob is stored on IPFS. A Stellar transaction carries the pointer to your recipient. Cost: 0.00001 XLM.

04
📩
Receive and decrypt

Recipient fetches the pointer, retrieves from IPFS, and decrypts locally. Only they can read it.
